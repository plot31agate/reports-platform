## The real architecture: two repos + per-client instances

The platform is delivered as **two connected codebases**, plus **one Client HQ
repo per client**:

1. **`reports-platform`** — the hub.
   - **Report** engine (the heavy monthly report) — LIVE.
   - **Agency HQ** — DF's cross-client ops console, and it **provisions the
     Client HQ portals** (via the `client-hq` skill / `scripts/hq_builder.py`
     / `/agency/api/.../build-hq`).
   - **Finance HQ** — internal, separate.

2. **`social-builder`** (+ per-client clones `daisy-social-builder`,
   `igs-social-builder`) — the **Client HQ portal**: the client-facing monthly
   loop, **one instance per client**, generated by the `client-hq` skill and
   deployed to a per-client cPanel folder. `social-builder` itself is the
   **YM Predictions** build.

So the platform is **not multi-tenant SaaS** — it's a hub plus **per-client
cloned Client HQ portals**. That is the single most important fact for both
building and commercialising (see "Implications").

### Notes that matter
- **Rooms are per-client toggles.** YM ships Dashboard · Planner · Studio ·
  Social(+Buffer) · plan-approval share · Reports · Setup(+Walkthrough).
  Content engine, Site Health and Strategy(PR) are built but **off** for YM
  (social-only client). Different clients enable different rooms.
- **Reporting stays in the hub.** Each Client HQ links to / logs the DF
  Reporting Platform report; the portal's own Reports room is lightweight.
- **Agency HQ ↔ Client HQ are connected.** Agency HQ builds and tracks the
  Client HQ instances — the provisioning path in `reports-platform`
  (`hq_builder.py`) produces `social-builder`-style repos.
- **Leads Central** is the one genuinely-unbuilt blueprint module. The
  WordPress libs in the portal (`wordpress-lib.php`) are **content publishing**,
  not lead capture / scoring / CRM. No lead-scoring, follow-up automation or
  CRM connect exists in either repo.

## Implications (this is the important bit)

1. **The build question isn't "build the loop" — it's "productise the
   delivery."** The loop exists; it's shipped as per-client cloned repos +
   cPanel deploys, brand-configured by hand/skill per client. The real gap for
   scaling is **multi-tenancy / repeatable, cheaper provisioning / self-serve**,
   not features.
2. **This validates the commercialisation plan.** The `client-hq` provisioning
   *is* the "Launchpad" (paid setup) mechanism from
   `docs/commercialisation-plan.md`; the per-client portal is the ongoing
   licence surface. The managed productised-service route fits what already
   exists.
3. **Only Leads Central needs building from scratch** to complete the blueprint
   — and per the commercialisation notes it's best treated as a separate
   WordPress track / front-door product.
4. **Per-client drift is a real risk.** `CLIENT.md` shows YM is an "older-clone
   portal" that lost the runtime `modules` toggle and needed manual re-pointing.
   Clones diverge — a productisation goal should be a single template + config,
   not N hand-maintained repos.

## Bottom line

The blueprint is ~90% built and in production per client. The work ahead is
**productising the per-client Client HQ into something repeatable/multi-tenant**,
enabling the right rooms per client, keeping reporting central in the hub, and
building **Leads Central** if/when it's wanted. See `docs/build-backlog.md`.
