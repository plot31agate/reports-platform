# Digital Footprints Client Platform — Sales Brief

**Product list & feature set for the marketing team** · September 2026
Working product names below are internal — marketing owns final naming.

---

## The one-liner

One platform that runs a client's entire marketing month — plan it, make it,
push it live, and prove it worked — with AI doing the heavy lifting and a
human approving every word before a client sees it.

Today's shipped core is the **Report** module (live at
reports.digital-footprints.co.uk). The rest of the monthly cycle — **Plan**,
**Make**, **Push**, plus **Client HQ** and the **Leads Central** WordPress
plugin — is the roadmap, and is packaged below as locked/upsell modules.

---

## Why this exists (the problem we sell against)

1. **Proof of value is the #1 reason clients churn.** Most agencies send a
   screenshot deck three weeks late. We send a branded, board-ready report —
   web page and PDF — built from live data, with plain-English commentary on
   what worked, what to watch, and what happens next month.
2. **Reporting eats the margin.** Pulling GA4, Search Console, Ahrefs, socials
   and press coverage into one document takes days per client per month. Our
   pipeline does it in minutes: sync, build, review, publish.
3. **Strategy and results live in different rooms.** Plans get written in
   January and never meet the numbers. The platform closes the loop: every
   report ends in recommended actions, and (roadmap) every month's plan starts
   from last month's results.
4. **Clients want one front door.** Not five dashboards and a shared drive.
   One branded portal: their reports, their plan, their sign-offs.

## Who it's for

**Primary buyer — the person who has to prove marketing works.**
Marketing directors, heads of growth and founders at ambitious brands in
competitive or regulated sectors (reference client: Sportingtech, iGaming).
They answer to a board or investors, juggle PR + SEO + social + content, and
need evidence, not vibes.

**Secondary audience — the client's wider team.** The portal, share links and
sign-off flows are built so a CEO, sales director or investor can open a
report from a link with no login and no training.

**Leads Central buyer — any client with a WordPress site and a sales team.**
Businesses whose site generates enquiries but whose follow-up is a shared
inbox. Sold as an add-on to retainer clients or standalone.

**Not the pitch:** self-serve SaaS for other agencies. The platform is the
machine behind *our* service — sell the outcome, not the software licence.

## What's live today: the Report module (the minimum client offer)

This is the "min client" package — sellable on its own, today.

### 1. Every channel, one report

**Feature:** 17 data sources feed one monthly report: media mentions and
press coverage, share of voice, GA4 traffic + geography + daily trends,
Search Console, core keyword rankings, Ahrefs authority and backlinks,
competitor benchmarks, LinkedIn, Facebook & Instagram, TikTok, influencer
activity, technical SEO, and leads.

**Benefit to the client:** the whole marketing picture in one place, monthly,
without chasing five tools or three suppliers. Sections are switched on per
client, so the report is *their* report — an iGaming brand and a B2B firm
don't get the same template.

### 2. Live connections, not copy-paste

**Feature:** direct API syncs to Ahrefs, Google Analytics 4, Google Search
Console (with automatic dual-route fallback), Meta (Facebook + Instagram) and
Google News mention harvesting. One click pulls the month; automated feeds
capture press mentions as they happen.

**Benefit:** the numbers are the numbers — pulled from source, not retyped.
Faster turnaround, no transcription errors, and coverage gets captured even
when nobody's watching.

### 3. AI analysis with a human signature

**Feature:** Claude reads every press mention and scores sentiment with the
client's industry context (it knows what a "good" story looks like in
iGaming). It then drafts the executive summary, per-section commentary,
"what worked / what to watch", and next month's actions bucketed into *lean
into / investigate / fix urgently*. An account manager reviews and can edit
every headline, number, tile and table row before publishing.

**Benefit:** senior-analyst insight at machine speed — but nothing reaches a
client unreviewed. The pitch line: **"AI-drafted, human-approved."** That's a
differentiator against both lazy dashboard tools and pure-AI reporting.

### 4. A report clients actually open

**Feature:** each report is a branded web page in the client's colours *and*
a print-quality PDF. Traffic-light "at a glance" strip up top, month-on-month
sparklines and daily trend charts throughout, and a closing "next month"
action plan.

**Benefit:** it forwards well. A CEO gets the verdict in ten seconds
(traffic lights), the marketing lead gets the depth, the board pack gets the
PDF. Reporting becomes a reason to renew, not a chore to excuse.

### 5. The client portal & sharing

**Feature:** every client gets a branded portal listing all their reports.
Invites are magic links — no passwords, instantly revocable. Time-limited
share links open a single report with no login at all. Every view is tracked
(who opened, when, web or PDF).

**Benefit:** zero-friction delivery, and *we know when they read it* — which
tells account managers who's engaged and warns us before a quiet client
becomes a churned one.

### 6. The machine behind the service (internal, but sell the outcome)

**Feature:** Agency HQ derives the week's tasks automatically from real state
— report due, coverage gap, connection error, client site down, approval
pending — and sweeps every client's live estate for health. A five-step
wizard (with Claude drafting the setup from a one-line brief) onboards a new
client in minutes. Credentials sit in an encrypted, audited vault.

**Benefit (client-facing translation):** nothing falls through the cracks.
Onboarding takes days, not weeks. "Your account is monitored every morning,
not just at month-end." Don't sell the tool; sell the reliability it creates.

## Locked & upsell modules (roadmap — sell as "coming", price as tiers)

These appear in the platform as locked tiles: visible, named, and one
conversation away. Each one has a natural upsell trigger.

### 🔒 Plan — strategy that answers to the numbers

- Reviews last month's report: recommended actions vs what actually happened.
- Suggests next month's strategy as **three committed things**, each with an
  outcome and tactics, accept/reject — informed by the client's notes and setup.
- "Plan Month" calendar: drag-and-drop, export, reminder pings.
- Shareable plan view for **client sign-off** before the month starts.

**Benefit:** strategy stops being a January document. Every month opens with
a plan built from evidence, and the client has skin in it because they signed
it off.
**Upsell trigger:** any client who asks "so what do we do about this?" at the
end of a report — the report already ends with actions; Plan turns them into
a committed calendar.

### 🔒 Make — Studio & Content

- **Studio:** creative production space — templates, reels and short-form
  video, a "you need…" checklist generated from the plan calendar that you
  tick off straight into the social queue.
- **Content:** ideas board, guided "what are you creating?" flow, drafting,
  review workflow, SEO check, imagery.

**Benefit:** the plan doesn't stall at "someone should make that". Everything
the month needs is listed, drafted, reviewed and queued in one place.
**Upsell trigger:** clients on reporting-only retainers whose bottleneck is
producing the content the report says they need.

### 🔒 Push — schedule and go live

- Scheduling and push-to-live for the social queue via Buffer (uses our
  existing subscription — no new tooling cost).
- Optional **client sign-off gate** before anything publishes.

**Benefit:** the loop closes inside one platform — planned in Plan, made in
Make, approved by the client, published by Push, measured in Report.
**Upsell trigger:** any client currently emailing us assets to "post when
you can", or nervous brands (regulated sectors) who need approval trails.

### ◐ Client HQ — the client's front door (portal live, dashboard next)

- **Live now:** branded portal, magic-link access, reports web + PDF, curated
  deliverables (e.g. a 12-month PR campaign calendar).
- **Roadmap:** Dashboard HQ home screen — report stats plus "this week
  needs", a stepped first-load setup walkthrough, and the Plan/Make/Push
  modules surfacing here as they ship (locked tiles until unlocked).

**Benefit:** one login for the client's entire marketing operation. Also the
platform's own best salesperson — every locked tile is a visible upgrade.

### 🔒 Leads Central — the WordPress leads add-on

A plugin for the client's own WordPress site:

- Monitor and **score leads** as they arrive; snapshot analysis.
- Manage follow-ups; **automated email follow-up** sequences.
- Connects to the client's **existing CRM** — augment, don't replace.
- Secure login, sales-tracking reporting, acquisition tracking — feeding lead
  numbers straight into the monthly report (the report already carries a
  leads metric today).

**Benefit:** turns the website from a brochure into a tracked sales channel,
and closes the last attribution gap — marketing activity → traffic → leads →
followed-up revenue, all in one monthly report.
**Upsell trigger:** every client on WordPress whose "leads" arrive in a
shared inbox. Sellable standalone as a foot-in-the-door product.
